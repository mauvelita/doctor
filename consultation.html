Private Consultations

Those interested in seeking treatment or discussing a potential procedure at Impossible Bodies are invited to submit a consultation request through the form below.

Please provide the necessary details regarding your inquiry, including the nature of the procedure you wish to discuss and any relevant medical history. All submissions are reviewed privately, and appointments are arranged at the clinic’s discretion.

Submission of a request does not guarantee an appointment. Prospective clients will be contacted should their inquiry be of interest to the doctor.

Your mail app will open with the clinic already set as the recipient. Press send there.

Your information will be handled with the utmost discretion.
