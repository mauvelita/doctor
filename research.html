Research

  • Reconstructive Surgery Complex surgical reconstruction involving damaged, altered, or extensively modified anatomical structures.
  • Biomechanics The study and application of mechanical principles to biological systems, with particular attention to movement, structural integrity, and physical performance.
  • Neural Integration Experimental interfaces connecting synthetic components with the nervous system, including the development of responsive prosthetic systems.
  • Synthetic Organ Systems Research into artificial replacements and supplementary systems designed to replicate or extend biological functions.
  • Human Augmentation Procedures intended to expand physical capabilities beyond conventional anatomical limitations.

Théoden’s current work focuses on the integration of biological tissue and synthetic structures. His research includes experimental implants, advanced prosthetics, and procedures involving extensive anatomical reconstruction.
