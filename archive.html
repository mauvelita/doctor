Education & Practice

Théoden received advanced training in surgery and biomechanics, specializing in reconstructive procedures. Despite his proficiency in conventional surgery, he developed an interest in experimental techniques involving unapproved materials and synthetic integrations. He began conducting procedures outside the scope of his hospital practice, none of which were entered into official medical records.

Board Action

Following an investigation into his unauthorized work, the medical board revoked his medical license. Théoden made no attempt to appeal the decision.

Private Practice

He subsequently established a private clinic in the unmapped tiers of the undercity. His practice developed through private referrals, primarily among wealthy clients and established circles with access to experimental medical technology. The facility operates independently of conventional medical institutions and is not subject to their regulatory oversight.

The clinic now operates on a commission basis. All prospective clients require a private introduction, and consultations are arranged directly through his office. No public appointments are offered.
