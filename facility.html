Facility

Location

The clinic occupies a secured floor within the unmapped tiers of the undercity. The facility is inaccessible through public routes and operates independently of registered medical institutions.

Its premises include private consultation rooms, surgical suites, diagnostic facilities, recovery rooms, and dedicated research laboratories.

The family research estate remains a separate location from the clinic — the estate marks where his research began; the clinic marks his independent medical practice.

Admission

The clinic accepts clients exclusively through private referrals. Prospective patients are introduced through established contacts and undergo an initial consultation before any procedure is considered.

Appointments are arranged directly through the administrative office. Patient records and procedural documentation are maintained internally.

Personnel

The clinic is managed by a small staff selected for their technical qualifications and professional discretion.

Théoden’s secretary oversees administration, correspondence, scheduling, and client introductions. A former colleague from his hospital career, he remained with Théoden following the revocation of his medical license.

The remaining personnel are responsible for clinical assistance, laboratory maintenance, diagnostics, and patient care. All staff operate under Théoden’s direct supervision.
