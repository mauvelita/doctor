Family Background

Théoden was born into a family established in medical research and biomechanics. His parents maintained a private research estate equipped with laboratories and facilities dedicated to experimental medicine and synthetic integration.

His upbringing was closely tied to their work. Exposed to anatomical research and surgical technology from an early age, he developed an interest in human physiology that eventually extended beyond conventional medical practice.

Early Development

His parents recognized his aptitude for the sciences and provided access to their research and resources. His education was supplemented by private instruction, with particular emphasis on anatomy, physiology, and biomechanics.

His interest in surgery developed alongside his understanding of the body’s mechanical and biological systems. What began as academic curiosity became a sustained interest in reconstructive techniques and the integration of synthetic components.

Family Enterprise

Following his formal medical training, Théoden continued developing the research his parents had established. His work eventually surpassed the scope of their original projects, prompting them to transfer control of the family enterprise to him.

Both parents remain supportive of his research. They have also participated in his experimental work, becoming among the earliest recipients of his advanced procedures.
